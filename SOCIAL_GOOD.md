# Social Good Policy // Mission-Linked Messaging

Ledger: 10 AC = $1 — Anom Coin IS currency, earns: +12 AC, +8 AC etc.

Social Good Score IS NOT currency. It is a score, separate from wallet.
- Earned by: missions, governance, guardian queue, world-building
- Not purchasable, not convertible, not spendable
- Displayed as: Score, not $, not AC
- Mission-linked messaging: "Complete mission → +10 Social Good Score → unlocks Guardian tools, not wallet"

Policy: Anom Coin wallet = commerce (Stripe). Social Good Score = reputation (missions).
